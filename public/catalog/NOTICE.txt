Australian food catalogue attribution
=====================================

This directory contains a transformed subset of AUSNUT 2023 from Food
Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ): food identity, classification,
derivation, household measures, and energy/protein/carbohydrate/fat/fibre
values.

Source: https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/science-data/food-nutrient-databases/ausnut/data-files
Dataset: AUSNUT 2023
Attribution: © Food Standards Australia New Zealand

The source database and this transformed database are distributed under the
FSANZ Data User Licence Agreement, based on Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Australia. This notice applies to the transformed
catalogue data and does not change the licence of the surrounding application
code.

Nutrition values are reference data, not individual nutritional or medical
advice. Derivation metadata is retained in each catalogue record.
